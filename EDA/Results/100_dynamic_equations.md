# Script 100 — Dynamically weighted numerical regression equations

Each expert is an independently fitted Ridge regression on NHANES 2005–08.

**Complete model:** $Y_i=\sum_{k=1}^{K}w_k(Z_i)(\beta_{0k}+\sum_{j=1}^{7}\beta_{jk}Z_{ij}+\gamma_k^\top C_i)+\varepsilon_i$.
The gate weights are train-only smooth functions of frozen physiological Z.

### Gate definitions

- A_axis: $p=\sigma(z^*_{A3}/\tau)$, weights $(1-p,p)$.
- G_axis: $p=\sigma(z^*_{G3}/\tau)$, weights $(1-p,p)$.
- AG_axes: independent A/G soft gates, 4 combinations.
- kmeans2: $w_k=\mathrm{softmax}_k(-\|z^*-\mu_k\|^2/(\tau d))$; centroids fitted on training physiology only.
- $z^*$ is standardized with the stored training gate mean and SD.
